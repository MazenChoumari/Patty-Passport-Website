FREQUENTLY ASKED

Do I need to book ahead?

Walk-ins are always welcome at the Quick Bite counter. For the Full Experience zones, birthdays, and groups of 6+, reserve your flight via the booking page.

How do passport stamps work?

One stamp per destination visited. 3 destinations unlocks a free drink, 5 a free side, 8 a secret destination burger, and all 21 unlocks the full World Traveller buffet. See Rewards.

Is there parking?

Yes — 40 to 50 surface spaces around the building, with signage for each route. Bus lines L1, L3 and L7 stop at "Parque Tecnológico – Patty Passport," a short walk from our entrance.

Can I get a Quick Bite without a full sit-down?

Yes — the Quick Bite counter has no minimum spend. Experience Zone seating carries a minimum of €10/adult and €8/child.

ALLERGENS & DIETARY INFO

Every item on our menu is tagged individually — Halal-friendly, Kosher-sensitive, No pork, Alcohol-free, Vegetarian, Vegan-friendly, Contains alcohol, Contains nuts, and more. We never assume one rule fits every guest; ask any Journey Crew member and they'll walk the full card with you.

Patty Passport is open to everyone: halal-friendly, kosher-sensitive, vegetarian, vegan, alcohol-free or alcohol-friendly. If you have a specific allergy not covered by our tags, tell your Check-In Host before ordering.

PRIVACY

This is a front-end prototype build — My Passport accounts, stamp history, and bookings made on this site are stored only in your browser (mock/local state) and are not sent to a real server or shared with third parties.

When Patty Passport World S.L. launches with a real backend, this page will be replaced with a full privacy policy covering data collected at check-in, loyalty accounts, and marketing communications, in line with EU GDPR.
