Private zone vs. group minimum

A flat hall fee of €80–120 books a zone for fully private use — that fee is on top of the group's food and drink, not credited toward it. If the zone isn't booked private, there's no hall fee, just a group minimum spend of €400+ total across the table.

WHAT THE EVENING ACTUALLY LOOKS LIKE

EVERY GUEST HAS A ROUTE.

Patty Passport is open to everyone — halal-friendly, kosher-sensitive, vegetarian, vegan, alcohol-free or alcohol-friendly. No guest needs to fit the menu; the menu is built to fit the table.

Clear information

Every menu tags pork, alcohol and dietary info directly on the item — nothing is assumed. Guests with serious or life-threatening allergies should confirm directly with the crew host before ordering; tags flag common concerns but don't replace that conversation.
